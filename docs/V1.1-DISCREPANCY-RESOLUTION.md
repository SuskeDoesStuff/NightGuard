# v1.1-alpha — Discrepancy resolution

Every discrepancy found while executing `docs/v1.1-PROMPT.md`, and how it was resolved. Items marked
**found during execution** were not in the prompt or the planning round.

**Status at the end:** `pytest` and `pytest -m slow` green, `ruff` clean, `mypy --strict` clean over
37 source files, `scripts/validate.py` reporting every criterion with its measured value.
**Five of §7's eight v1.1 exit criteria are met; three are reported as misses.** No threshold was
moved and no run was tuned until a number appeared.

| # | Criterion | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `gym.make` for every §2.4 ID | **Met** |
| 2 | A rising curve on stage 1, single config, before sweeping | **Met** — `mean_steps` 268.7 → 830.9 |
| 3 | Beats `do_nothing` on nights 4, 5, 6 at n=500 | **Night 4 only**, 0.140 vs 0.000 at 9.02σ |
| 4 | Beats `rhythm` on night 6 | **Not met, not attempted** |
| 5 | Oracle gap non-zero, as a lower bound | **Not met** — −0.0040 ± 0.0040 at −1.00σ, a floor effect rather than a leak |
| 6 | Duty cycle logged, trend either way | **Met** — 1.000 → 0.002 across four arms |

| 8 | Four §11 gates green, spec matches code | **Met** |

`validate.py` runs **94 checks**; the four failures above are the only ones.

The honest one-line summary: **the harness, the measurements and the discipline are sound; the
policy is not.** Nothing trained here learned to survive a night on the curriculum's first stage.
What the milestone produced instead is a characterised account of why, and three findings nobody
had on their list.

## 3. Decisions taken in planning

| Decision | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Which env IDs to register | **Two.** `NightGuard-v0` takes `night` as a kwarg, so SB3's `env_kwargs` covers the six shipped nights without six IDs; `NightGuard-CustomMax-v0` exists because stage 3 is not one of them. **No `max_episode_steps`** on either: the night is a fixed horizon the simulator ends itself, and a `TimeLimit` would report termination as truncation and change the final bootstrap |
| Where the Oracle pair runs | **On the hardest config where the base agent demonstrably learns**, chosen from the stage results rather than fixed in advance. Criterion 5 reads a zero gap as evidence of a leak, and both a ceiling (night 5, where `rhythm` alone reaches 0.973) and a floor would manufacture a spurious zero |
| Torch build | The **default CUDA build**, then benchmarked against CPU rather than assumed. The GPU measured *slower* — see §4 |
| Hyperparameters | A frozen `TrainConfig` with YAML override, per CLAUDE.md's no-constants-in-logic invariant. Values fixed from a throughput measurement taken **before any training run**; the prompt's ban is on sweeping to chase a result, and a configuration that cannot finish is not a configuration |

## 4. Found during execution

| # | Discrepancy | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | **`sparse_mode` is not gradient-free.** The prompt states, and the plan pre-registered, that stage 1 under `sparse_mode` "will produce no gradient at all". It reached `mean_steps` **723.6** — the same plateau the dense arm found — with its undiscounted mean return pinned at −10.00 throughout | **Reported, not corrected.** The reasoning missed the discount: a death at step `t` is worth `-10 · γ^t` at the episode start, which *increases* with `t`, so delaying death raises the discounted objective with every dense term switched off. Worth **+2.6 of return** between dying at step 269 and at 684, at `γ = 0.999`. The narrower claim survives: `sparse_mode` removes the *shaped* signal, leaving one that is weaker, more delayed, and a function of the discount factor rather than of §6.3. §7's preamble reworded to say that instead of repeating the stronger claim |
| E1 | **The first unseeded reset replayed the seeded episode.** `NightGuardEnv.reset` built its own `default_rng(seed)` on the seeded path but used `self.np_random` on the unseeded one. Gymnasium seeds `self.np_random` from `SeedSequence(seed)`, which is bit-identical — verified: same bit-generator state, same spawned children — so the first unseeded reset spawned substreams 0..5 a second time. Measured on night 6 under all-`NOOP`, episodes 1 and 2 were both `(102 ticks, KILLED_SPRINTER, 92.253)`. **SB3 seeds once and then resets without a seed forever after**, so every training run and every naive 500-episode evaluation duplicated its first episode | Always spawn from `self.np_random`. Exactly equivalent on the seeded path, so `reset(seed=k)` still matches `NightSim.from_seed(k)` and no fixture moves; `validate.py` still passes 81 of 81. Regression guard added in both `validate.py` and `test_env_api.py`, with the §8.0 non-vacuity check that the outcomes actually differ |
| E2 | **The GPU is slower than the CPU**, by a third: 169 against 251 training steps/s | At `n_envs=16` and 100 input dims the LSTM kernels are too small to amortise dispatch. Recorded rather than assumed, and the runs use CPU. The CUDA build was still installed, so the comparison is a measurement rather than an argument |
| E3 | **v1.0's open vectorisation question, settled.** §5 of the v1.0 discrepancy doc left `env/vector.py` unbuilt by decision and asked v1.1 to check it against a real training loop | `scripts/profile_training.py` measures the split directly: the environment is **0.7% of training wall clock**, and the PPO update is **40× the rollout collection**. `env/vector.py` stays unbuilt, now on a measurement. §6.5 updated, with the object-versus-vectorised equivalence gate retained for whenever it is built |
| E2a | **Criterion 5 is not answerable at floor performance.** The matched pair gave a gap of **−0.0040 ± 0.0040**, 1.00σ — three survivors against one out of 500. §7 reads a zero gap as either a leaking observation or a task that needs no memory | **Neither, and the criterion's phrasing must not force one.** v1.0's probe covered 33 hidden-state mutations and `validate.py`'s four no-leak checks plus the non-vacuity converse still pass. Both arms are pinned at the floor, where the comparison measures optimisation noise rather than information — the floor effect the planning round predicted when choosing the config, which is why the check also reports `mean_steps`. That went the wrong way too. The arms are genuinely different policies (125 PROWLER deaths against 18, at a quarter the duty cycle); ground truth changed behaviour and not survival, because both die to power. The stop condition is deliberately **not** triggered: stopping would assert a leak four independent checks say is absent |
| E2b | **The stage saved the last policy, not the best.** The `normalised` arm reached survival **0.1200** with a positive return and a duty cycle of 0.0702 at 700,000 steps, then collapsed; its 2,000,000-step weights score 0.0000 with 500/500 SPRINTER deaths, and the good policy is unrecoverable | A harness defect, not a result. `EvaluationCallback` now checkpoints the best evaluation to `best.zip`, ranked on survival with `mean_steps` breaking ties, and `StageResult` records both paths. On a run this unstable the final weights are an arbitrary sample of an oscillation. Every figure in this milestone predates the fix and none is restated by it |
| E2c | **`target_kl` did not prevent the collapse**, which was the hypothesis the `normalised` arm existed to test | Reported as the negative result it is. The arm still fell from 823.1 mean steps to 168.6 inside 200,000 steps. So the instability is not simply an unguarded update, and "the baseline was under-configured" is at best a partial explanation — reward normalisation bought the only real survival anybody saw, and bought no stability at all |
| E2d | **My own criterion-5 check could not fail for the right reason.** It passed on any non-zero gap, so it passed on the negative, statistically-null result actually measured, printing `-0.004` beside a green PASS | Rewritten to require a **positive** gap of at least 2σ; it now fails at −1.00σ. This is v0.3's shadowed-validation-function trap one level up: the check ran, printed a number, and said PASS. §8.0 exists for exactly this |
| E2e | **`-dirty` says a run is unreproducible but not how far.** Two of three runs were dirty in documentation only, and nothing in the artefact could demonstrate that | The manifest now records `dirty_paths` alongside the SHA, so "reproducible apart from the prose" is checkable rather than asserted. The v1.1 runs predate the fix and keep the weaker guarantee, which is recorded rather than glossed |

## 4a. The result, and the criteria it does not meet

Stage 1, night 5, 2,000,000 steps, one configuration, 500 held-out seeds:

| Policy | Survival | `mean_steps` | Duty | Dominant cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `do_nothing` | 0.000 | 172.2 | 0.000 | SPRINTER |
| `monitor_down` | 0.000 | 820.4 | 0.000 | blackout, 200/200 |
| **learned, dense** | **0.0060** | **824.9** | 0.162 | blackout, 438/500 |
| **learned, sparse** | **0.0000** | **723.6** | 0.029 | blackout, 500/500 |
| `rhythm` | 0.980 | 1069.3 | 0.083 | survived |

The agent rediscovers `monitor_down` and stops there — 824.9 steps against 820.4, and the same
power-bound failure in 88% of episodes. `mean_steps` rises from 268.7 to 830.9 and the duty cycle
falls from 1.000 to 0.002, so criterion 2 is met; neither is survival.

The plateau is structural rather than an optimiser artefact. Escaping it needs the sequence *close
left → close right → raise monitor → drop → reopen*, and every prefix of it is strictly worse than
not starting: raising the monitor with a door open is exactly what admits an entity (§3.4, §3.5),
and closing doors alone spends power for nothing. §6.3's power term charges the first step
immediately; the payoff arrives hundreds of steps later.

**The one clean pass came by transfer.** Evaluated on nights it never trained on, the night-5 policy
scores **0.140 on night 4** against `do_nothing`'s 0.000 — a 9.02σ margin — while scoring 0.006 on
night 5 and 0.000 on night 6. That is what the diagnosis predicts rather than a contradiction of it:
§3.10 makes `night_constant = 0.1 / night_divisor`, so night 4 (divisor 4.0) drains less than
night 5 (3.0), and a policy whose only failure mode is running out of power converts that headroom
directly into survival. The agent did not learn to *manage* power; it learned a fixed posture that
fits inside night 4's budget and not night 5's.

**No threshold was moved to make anything pass.** Criterion 3's bar — beating `do_nothing` by a
margin outside sampling error at n=500 — is met on night 4 and missed on night 5: 0.0060 against
0.000 is 1.74σ, under the 2σ the check requires. Reported as a partial miss rather than rounded up.
